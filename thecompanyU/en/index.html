THE COMPANY U is a K-beauty specialist distributing premium beauty, healthcare, and pet cosmetics brands to global markets. Shortly after its founding, the company completed major global certifications and is rapidly expanding its presence through participation in the world's four leading trade fairs.

Founding & Certifications

Since its founding in 2024, the company has secured a trusted infrastructure by completing key global certifications including FDA (US) and CPNP (Europe) within a short period.

Multichannel D2C

Scale-up Goal

Built on a stable revenue base and differentiated brand assets, the company aims to grow into a leader in the global beauty and healthcare market.

Why NILONICA Is Different from 25,000+ K‑Beauty Products

A Rare, Little‑Known Shea

Everyone has heard of shea butter — few know this one. NILONICA uses a rare, naturally occurring shea species found only along the Nile River.

Process Over Ingredient

What matters more than a good raw material is how it's processed. No boiling, no melting — only cold‑press organic processing, certified by France's Eco‑cert Organic and the USDA Organic standard.

Maximum, Optimal Recipe

Even the best ingredient falls flat in a small dose. NILONICA formulates at the maximum, most optimal concentration — and names each product after that very percentage.

Clinically Verified Efficacy

Not a claim we make ourselves — efficacy confirmed through skin clinical trials for deep moisture, itch relief, skin barrier improvement, skin regeneration, and redness improvement.

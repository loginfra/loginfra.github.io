THE COMPANY U

THE COMPANY U is a K-beauty specialist distributing premium beauty, healthcare, and pet cosmetics brands to global markets. Shortly after its founding, the company completed major global certifications and is rapidly expanding its presence through participation in the world's four leading trade fairs.

Founding & Certifications

Since its founding in 2024, the company has secured a trusted infrastructure by completing key global certifications including FDA (US) and CPNP (Europe) within a short period.

Multichannel D2C

Building a comprehensive online and offline global distribution network including its own mall, Naver, Coupang, Hyundai Hmall, and Amazon.

Scale-up Goal

Built on a stable revenue base and differentiated brand assets, the company aims to grow into a leader in the global beauty and healthcare market.

Global Growth Roadmap

A solid global expansion scenario spanning from the Landing to the Expand phase

2024 - LANDING (Foundation)

  • THE COMPANY U incorporated and certified as a women-owned business
  • Launch of Dr.Frenshea pet cosmetics products
  • Multi-Gyn feminine wash supplied to women's hospitals and sold online
  • Regular donation activities to pet shelters begin

2025 - GEARING UP (Acceleration)

  • Launch of NILONICA premium skincare brand
  • Regulatory registration and FDA certification progressing in Japan, Vietnam, Thailand, and the US
  • First export achieved to the Russian market
  • New product crowdfunding exceeds 800% of target
  • Participation in major global exhibitions including New York, Japan, Las Vegas, and France

2026 - EXPAND (Structural Completion)

  • Securing revenue momentum for global expansion and structural growth
  • Full-scale expansion into key markets including Russia, the US, and China
  • Expansion into global e-commerce platforms such as Amazon and Shopee
  • Pursuing entry into major domestic retail channels, duty-free shops, and department stores
  • Participation in global B2B trade exhibitions four times a year
